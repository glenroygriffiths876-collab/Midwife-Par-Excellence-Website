Meet Simone

Simone Cooper-Bernard — the woman behind Midwife Par Excellence.

Simone Cooper-Bernard is a Jamaican registered midwife and health educator whose public education work focuses on pregnancy, antenatal care, childbirth, reproductive health and family planning.

Midwife Par Excellence is Simone's maternal and reproductive-health education brand — a space for practical information that helps women and families prepare, ask better questions and feel more confident navigating care.
